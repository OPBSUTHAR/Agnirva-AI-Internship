## 5. What This Week Is About (§01)

**Question answered:** What exactly is the need, and how will we know when we have addressed it?

That sounds simple. It is not. Most projects fail not from lack of talent or effort, but because the need was never defined precisely enough for the team to align on what they were building.

**Five working days (Mon–Fri):** Define problem statement, stakeholders, objectives, scope, assumptions, risks, success metrics. Assemble these into the **eleven standard sections of a Business Requirements Document**. By Friday, assemble a finished BRD that becomes the reference for every other role week.

**Workload:** **30 artifact activities** across the week, **6 per day, Monday through Friday.** Days 1–4 build the BRD section by section. Day 5 is final assembly and handoff. Reading articles, AI guides, and concept reviews each day equip you to do that day's artifact activities well.

## 6. Pipeline Position (§02) — Role Focus 1 of 6

Chain runs:

```
Need (W2 BRD) → Landscape (W3 RLD) → Quality (W4 VQRD) → Product (W5 PRD) → Audience (W6 SCRP) → Language (W7 LAAP) → Synthesis (W8)
```

- **W2 BRD feeds every subsequent week directly:**
  - W3 SWOT assesses the landscape around the need you defined here
  - W5 PRD designs the product that meets the requirements you specify here
  - W7 Localization Kit adapts content for audiences you identify here
- **Implication:** Take this week seriously. The five days of work will be the spine of every artefact until the capstone.
